About the School Gaming League

Khali League is a school-focused gaming platform designed to bring students and schools together through organised competitive gaming. Students can register for several games in one registration.

Represent Your School

Register with your school details and compete as part of your school gaming community.

Multiple Games

Select every game you want to participate in instead of submitting a separate registration for each game.

Student-Focused

The registration collects the student and school information needed for league organisation.
